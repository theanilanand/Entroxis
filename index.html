Meet the founder

Anil Anand founded Entroxis to build the software growing businesses keep putting off: a proper website, an app customers actually use, a CRM the team trusts, and AI that does real work.

He studies Integrated MSc Physics at the Sardar Vallabhbhai National Institute of Technology, Surat (NIT Surat, also known as SVNIT). Physics is where the name comes from. Entropy describes how systems drift into disorder, and good engineering is the work of holding structure against that drift.

Anil is also the founder and CEO of WavyGo Mobility Pvt. Ltd., a DPIIT-recognized startup pre-incubated at the ASHINE programme at SVNIT with SSIP support. Building a product of his own shapes how Entroxis works with clients: keep the scope tight, show progress early and fix what users actually run into.

Why the name Entroxis

Entropy is what happens when a system drifts into disorder. Most growing businesses feel it: scattered sheets, missed follow-ups, tools that do not talk to each other.

We build the opposite. Clean structure that keeps working as your team, your customers and your data grow.

The logo says the same thing: loose bars, cut by one clean line.
